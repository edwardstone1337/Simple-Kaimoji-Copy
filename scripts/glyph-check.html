Glyph check

Open this in every browser and OS you care about and look for empty boxes. Font availability differs by platform, so a kaomoji that renders here may not render elsewhere.

Why this is a visual check and not an automated one. Automated detection was tried and does not work reliably. Comparing advance widths fails because a tofu box and a full-width CJK glyph are the same width, so it flags most of the character set. Comparing canvas pixels fails the other way: canvas composes combining marks differently from the DOM, so it reports everything as fine. The real failure mode here is a base character the font has, carrying combining marks it cannot compose, and only your eyes catch that dependably.

Highlighted cells use scripts a CJK and Latin font stack may not cover (Arabic, Thai, Kannada, Tibetan, Canadian syllabics and similar), often with stacked combining marks. They are the ones most likely to break, and are listed first so you can scan them quickly.
